# Physical Security and Social Engineering

## Physical Security Testing

Physical security testing evaluates controls that protect facilities,
equipment, and restricted areas.

### Key Components

#### Perimeter Security

Assess physical boundaries and controls intended to prevent unauthorized
entry.

#### Access Control

Review mechanisms such as:

- Locks
- Badges
- Electronic access systems
- Entry procedures

#### Personnel Security

Security awareness and staff procedures can influence physical security.

## Physical Testing Methodology

A physical assessment can examine:

- Social-engineering opportunities
- Locks and physical controls
- Electronic access systems
- Visitor procedures
- Physical barriers

### Legal and Ethical Requirements

Physical testing must have explicit authorization and clearly defined
boundaries.

## Social Engineering

Social engineering tests human behavior and organizational processes
rather than only technical controls.

### Psychological Principles

The material emphasizes that social-engineering techniques rely on human
behavior and trust.

### Common Techniques

#### Phishing

Attempts to influence a target through deceptive communication.

#### Spear Phishing

A more targeted form of phishing directed at a specific person or group.

#### Pretexting

Using a fabricated scenario or identity to influence a target.

#### Baiting

Using an appealing lure to encourage a target to take an action.

#### Physical Social Engineering

Uses social interaction in a physical environment to test access
controls or procedures.

## Conducting an Assessment

### 1. Reconnaissance

Understand the authorized target, people, locations, and relevant
procedures.

### 2. Scenario Development

Create a controlled scenario aligned with the agreed objectives.

### 3. Authorization and Documentation

Document the scenario, scope, permissions, limitations, and reporting
requirements.

## Ethical Considerations

Social engineering can affect people directly, so testing should
minimize unnecessary harm and embarrassment.

The tester must follow:

- Authorization
- Scope
- Rules of engagement
- Privacy requirements
- Safety procedures

## Key Takeaway

Physical and social-engineering assessments can reveal weaknesses that
technical testing may miss, but they require particularly careful
authorization, planning, and professional conduct.
